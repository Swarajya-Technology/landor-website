**Light of Landour**

 

A Beacon of shining Light that brings to the entire world the essence of the Lovely Landour. Designed to awaken your senses and bring to you the joys of being in pure nature harmony and holistic well-being.

Discover an intimate boutique resort nestled in the serene embrace of the captivating Landour mountains, near the charming town of Mussoorie. Welcome to the tranquil haven of Light of Landour, where you can indulge in a blissful retreat amidst breathtaking landscapes and abundant greenery.

Immersed in the positive energies that surround the Garhwal mountains, we are inspired by the ancient wisdom of Sanatana Dharma and Yoga. Our gracefully-designed accommodations are a testament to this inspiration, seamlessly blending the elements of nature, the energy centers of chakras, and the harmonizing balance of energy mudras.

Step into our mellow-toned spaces adorned with sophisticated interiors and enjoy the utmost comfort of our cozy bedding. Each room and suite is thoughtfully appointed with luxurious amenities, while a private balcony invites you to unwind and soak in the tranquility of the surroundings.

At Light of Landour, we invite you to embark on a journey of rejuvenation, where the convergence of nature's elements, the wisdom of chakras, and the harmony of mudras create an ambiance of serenity and well-being.

###  Built On Sustainability 

Embracing the ethos of sustainability, our resort is carefully crafted to intertwine with lush greenery, fostering a design philosophy that promotes wellness and environmental consciousness. We prioritize preserving the natural landscape, allowing trees to thrive, and enveloping our properties in a harmonious embrace.

Whether it's a secluded retreat amidst untouched forests or an urban oasis seamlessly blending greenery into its design, our resort exemplifies the integration of nature's elements. Native plants and biophilic design principles create an aesthetically pleasing environment while enhancing the human connection with the natural world, reducing stress, and nurturing overall well-being.

From the exterior adorned with abundant vegetation to the upcoming verdant landscapes, every aspect of our hotel immerses you in the heart of nature. Experience the soothing embrace of the environment, as our sustainable approach creates a tranquil haven where you can truly reconnect with the beauty and serenity of the natural world.
