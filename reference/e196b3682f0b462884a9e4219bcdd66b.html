Our Genesis & Mountain Roots

Awakening Through Sacred Wisdom & Primordial Pines

Discover an intimate boutique sanctuary nestled in the serene embrace of the captivating Landour ridge, just above Mussoorie. Immersed in the positive currents that drape the Garhwal peaks, Light of Landour was envisioned as a tranquil haven where modern souls can indulge in a restorative pause.

We draw boundless energy from the ancient traditions of Sanatana Dharma and Yogic science. Each suite, courtyard, and timber veranda is deliberately shaped to harmonize the physical body with cosmic vibrations, inviting guests to release mental static and welcome stillness.

Ecological Stewardship

Built On Sustainability • In Deep Reverence to the Forest

Embracing the ethos of conscious sustainability, our resort is carefully crafted to intertwine with lush greenery, fostering a design philosophy that promotes wellness and environmental harmony. We prioritize preserving the natural landscape, allowing ancient deodars to thrive unhindered, and enveloping our structures in a gentle embrace.

Native flora and biophilic design principles create an aesthetically grounding environment while enhancing the human connection with the natural world—reducing cortisol, heightening sensory perception, and nurturing long-term vitality.
