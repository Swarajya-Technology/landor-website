## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the contemplative stillness and understated grandeur of an alpine Himalayan sanctuary. Designed for discernment-driven travelers, wellness seekers, and literature-loving retreatants, the aesthetic channels misty deodar forests, raw slate stone, cedar smoke, and golden dusk light.

The emotional core is one of profound deceleration, grounding serenity, and artisanal hospitality. The interface merges:
- **Refined Minimalism:** Generous negative space evoking pure mountain air, deliberate restraint, and uncluttered composition.
- **Tactile Himalayan Elegance:** Subtle textural layering, linen-tinted warm backdrops, and muted precious-metal accents reminiscent of brass singing bowls and handcrafted joinery.
- **Editorial Typography:** High-character literary serif headings paired with clean, utilitarian architectural sans-serif elements to ground room specifications, wellness itineraries, and booking utilities.

Avoid superficial luxury tropes like glossy gold gradients, stark clinical whites, or aggressive sales notifications. The experience should feel like opening a bespoke, linen-bound guest journal in a mountaintop library.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the Garhwal Himalayan ecosystem: deodar canopies, wet slate paths, raw cedar bark, and mountain mist.

- **Primary (`#1B3B2B` - Deep Pine):** Represents the ancient pine and deodar forests of Landour. Used for focal call-to-action buttons, high-priority interactive components, navigation bars, and primary badges.
- **Secondary (`#C59B27` - Cedar Amber):** Evokes the glow of brass lanterns, warm cedar resin, and evening sun. Reserved for subtle borders, active booking indicators, ratings, package highlights, and delicate accent dividers.
- **Tertiary (`#161A1D` - Himalayan Slate):** A rich, organic charcoal stone hue that replaces harsh synthetic blacks. Used for high-contrast typography, structural footers, dialog overlays, and crisp icons.
- **Neutral (`#FBF9F5` - Linen Cream):** A soft, tactile, low-eyestrain surface reminiscent of unbleached handmade paper and misty mornings. Serves as the global backdrop.
- **Sub-surfaces (`#F4EFEA`):** Used for interior card containers, accommodation panels, and interactive input troughs.

Contrast is maintained by strictly pairing deep forest pine and slate typography over linen surfaces, keeping amber gold reserved for secondary accents with compliant background pairings.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy combines the classical, literary resonance of **EB Garamond** with the architectural precision of **Manrope**.

- **Editorial Serifs (EB Garamond):** Used for storytelling, overarching property names, suite narratives, and poetic section kickers. EB Garamond evokes Landour's rich literary heritage, Ruskin Bond-esque fireside prose, and quiet contemplation. Headings employ modest tracking with fluid proportions.
- **Clean Architectural Modernism (Manrope):** Handles all transactional components, booking bars, dates, availability markers, specs, and body copy. Manrope ensures effortless readability across screen sizes and environmental lighting.
- **Overline / Eyebrow Text:** Set in `label-sm` or `label-md` using Manrope in all-caps with generous letter-spacing (`0.16em` to `0.2em`) and Cedar Gold tinting to softly frame upcoming narratives.
- **Narrative Lead Body:** Employs `body-lg` in EB Garamond at 20px with a relaxed 32px line height, providing a magazine-like immersion for sanctuary packages and wellness philosophies.

## Layout & Spacing

The retreat layout embodies breathability, mirroring the vast openness of the Himalayan horizons. It relies on a generous 12-column responsive fluid grid capped at `1320px` to maintain intimate reading measures.

### Breakpoints & Grid Rules
- **Desktop (≥1200px):** 12 columns, `gutter-desktop` (32px), side margins of `minmax(2rem, 1fr)`. Hero and sanctuary presentation sections use expansive vertical rhythms (`space-4xl` to `space-5xl`) between thematic blocks.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns, `gutter-tablet` (24px), page margins `1.5rem`. Editorial side-by-side suites collapse into structured stacked layouts.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4 columns, `gutter-mobile` (16px), page margins `1rem`. Spacing contracts organically to `space-2xl` between sections to preserve flow.

### Booking Bar Layout
The horizontal persistent booking bar on desktop uses a clean single-row grid dividing dates, room tier, guests, and the action button. On mobile, it pins to the bottom viewport edge as an ergonomic, elevation-shielded persistent pill showing current best rates and a single tap trigger.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth follows the physical metaphor of mountain morning light—quiet, diffuse, and unhurried. Heavy, direct drop shadows are strictly avoided. Depth is established through **tonal layering**, **micro-borders**, and **diffused ambient illumination**.

### Depth Layers
- **Surface 0 (Canvas):** Base background in `#FBF9F5` (Linen Base). Completely flat.
- **Surface 1 (Cards & Accommodation Modules):** `#F4EFEA` (Linen Subtle) with a crisp 1px hairline border in `#E5DED5`. No shadow in rest state.
- **Surface 2 (Hovered Cards & Dropdowns):** Elevated using an ambient, dual-layer shadow:
  `box-shadow: 0 10px 30px -10px rgba(22, 26, 29, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(27, 59, 43, 0.03);`
  The border transitions softly to `#C59B27` at 40% opacity.
- **Surface 3 (Floating Booking Bar & Quick Inquiries):** Soft linen surface with heavy backdrop blur:
  `backdrop-filter: blur(16px); background: rgba(251, 249, 245, 0.88);`
  Bounded by `box-shadow: 0 16px 40px -12px rgba(22, 26, 29, 0.08)`.
- **Surface 4 (Modals & Custom Itinerary Builder):** Centered card atop an authentic Himalayan Slate scrim:
  `background: rgba(22, 26, 29, 0.65); backdrop-filter: blur(8px);`
  Modal container features crisp 1px framing in `#E5DED5` with `0 24px 64px -16px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.22)`.

## Shapes

The retreat aesthetic balances the structured geometry of mountain architecture and cedar window frames with soft tactile touches. The system uses a **Soft (Level 1)** roundedness baseline:

- **Standard Base Components (`rounded` / 4px):** Applied to form text inputs, select triggers, booking summary chips, and editorial image frames. Conveys quiet craftsmanship without sterile boxiness.
- **Containers & Retreat Cards (`rounded-lg` / 8px):** Applied to suite display cards, culinary panels, package feature blocks, and modal windows.
- **Interactive Badges & Quick Filters (`rounded-full`):** Category tags (e.g., *Mudra*, *Chakra*, *Ayurveda*) and floating social badges use pill shapes to contrast against the architectural grid.
- **Borders:** Consistent hairline borders of `1px solid #E5DED5`, providing crisp definition without visual heaviness.

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary Action (e.g., "Book Now", "Reserve Sanctuary"):** Solid `#1B3B2B` background with `#FBF9F5` typography. Height: 48px. Padding: 0 28px. Radius: 4px. Subtle hover transform: background lifts to `#2C4C3A` with a 1px amber baseline underline.
- **Secondary Action (e.g., "Get Personalized Plan", "Explore Packages"):** Outlined with 1.5px `#1B3B2B` or `#C59B27` over transparent canvas. Text matches border tone. Hover triggers gentle fill with 8% opacity tint.
- **Text Link CTAs (e.g., "[View Restaurants]", "[Read More]"):** Manrope label font (`label-md`), uppercase with `0.16em` letter-spacing, accompanied by an understated right arrow glyph (`→`) that moves 3px right on hover.

### Booking Bar
- A monolithic horizontally unified strip. Dividers between check-in, check-out, suite selector, and guests are rendered in 1px `#E5DED5`.
- Labels use `label-sm` in `#1B3B2B` muted to 60% opacity; selected values use `body-md` in `#161A1D` with medium weight.
- Rightmost block houses the primary CTA button, aligning cleanly with the input boxes.

### Retreat Cards (Accommodations & Healing Packages)
- **Structure:** Aspect ratio 16:10 or 4:3 photography with overflow hidden, framed above a padded container in `#F4EFEA`.
- **Content:** An overline category indicator (e.g., `INSPIRED BY MUDRAS`), followed by an `EB Garamond` title (`headline-md`), brief contemplative prose, a 3-point amenity grid (balcony view, king cedar bed, herbal bath), and a discreet price/night indicator.
- **Hover behavior:** Image gently zooms (1.03 scale over 800ms ease), border warms to Cedar Gold.

### Form Inputs & Inquiry Fields
- Text inputs and textareas sit on `#F4EFEA` or clean `#FFFFFF` with a 1px `#E5DED5` border.
- Height: 48px for standard inputs; padding: 0 16px.
- Focus state: Border transitions smoothly to `#1B3B2B` with zero ring glow, accompanied by a discreet 1px golden accent dot inside the field indicator.
- Required labels feature an amber asterisk (`*` in `#C59B27`).

### Chips & Badges
- Wellness taxonomy badges (e.g., *Heart*, *Soul*, *Body*, *Chakra*) use pill styling with 4px vertical, 12px horizontal padding.
- Neutral state: Tinted `#F4EFEA` background with `#1B3B2B` text and hairline border.
- Active/Selected state: Solid `#1B3B2B` background with `#FBF9F5` text.

### Modal & Itinerary Inquiry Dialog
- Floating modal for custom trip planning ("Make Your Package").
- Dismiss trigger (`×` close glyph) positioned at top-right in 20px Manrope with high contrast and generous touch target (44x44px).
- Internal layout features a two-column division on desktop: left pane showcases sanctuary guarantees (pure mountain air, silence hours, organic cuisine), right pane handles the multi-step request form.
